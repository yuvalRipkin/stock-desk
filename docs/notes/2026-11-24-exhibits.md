# Experiment 007: excluding exhibit pages (2026-11-24)

**Idea.** A 10-K PDF from FinanceBench includes its attached exhibits: compensation plans,
agreements, subsidiary lists. They are not where FinanceBench's answers are, but their text is
close enough to financial questions to win vector-search slots. With page expansion capped at
5 pages, every slot an exhibit takes is a 10-K page that is not sent.

**Method.** `EXCLUDE_EXHIBITS=true` drops pages at or after each filing's exhibit cutoff from
the vector search (a `page < cutoff` filter in the PGVectorStore query, so k hits are still
returned). The cutoff is the first page after the SIGNATURES page that opens with "Exhibit N",
not simply the page after the signatures: Amcor's Schedule II and American Express's statistical
appendix come between the two and are 10-K content.

| Filing | Pages | Cutoff | Exhibit pages | Max gold page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AMD 2022 | 121 | 108 | 13 | 57 |
| American Express 2022 | 260 | 175 | 85 | 154 |
| Boeing 2022 | 190 | 137 | 53 | 112 |
| PepsiCo 2022 | 503 | 130 | 373 | 77 |
| Amcor 2023 | 156 | 117 | 39 | 63 |

No gold evidence page of the 30 questions is at or after its cutoff. In 005, 4 of 30
questions had exhibit pages among their 5 (6 of 150 pages); PepsiCo, 74% exhibits, had most.

**Result** ([results](../../results/007_expand_p5_noexhibits.json), 30 questions × 3 runs,
`exclude_exhibits: true` in the recorded config):

| | 005 (5 pages) | 006 (8 pages) | 007 (5 pages, no exhibits) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence hit | 56.7% | 63.3% | **63.3%** |
| Page hit | 53.3% | 63.3% | 60.0% |
| Context per question | ~15,200 chars | ~22,500 chars | **~15,500 chars** |
| Accuracy | 54.4% ± 5.1 | 56.7% ± 3.3 | 54.4% ± 3.8 |

- **Evidence hit +2 questions over 005, equal to 006,** with 31% less context than 006 (and
  about 1% more than 005). Evidence hit is deterministic, so the gain is real.
- **Accuracy is unchanged** within the ±7-point noise floor
  ([noise-floor note](2026-11-24-noise-floor.md)); it neither confirms nor contradicts.
- **The change is exactly where expected.** Retrieval differs from 005 in 5 questions: the 4
  that had exhibit pages in 005 (00476, 00735, 01009, 01028) and 00720, whose vector search
  varies between runs on its own. Both evidence gains (00476, 01009) are among the 4; nothing
  was lost.

## financebench_id_01009 (the target case)

PepsiCo question with gold pages 3 and 4. In 005 its five pages were `[31, 488, 489, 70, 477]`,
three of them exhibits; at k=10 without the filter, 7 of 10 chunks were exhibit pages. With
exclusion: `[31, 70, 3, 68, 4]`, both gold pages, and correct 3/3 (005: 1/3, repeat 005b: 0/3).

## financebench_id_01964 (an apparent regression that is not one)

American Express, largest liability on the 2022 balance sheet; gold: customer deposits, page 97.
Correct 3/3 in 005 and 2/3 in 005b, but 0/3 in 007. Neither retrieval nor the cutoff explains
it:

- **Retrieval is identical in 005, 005b and 007:** pages `[125, 151, 124, 98, 138]`, the same
  12,828 characters of context. Page 97 is far below American Express's cutoff (175) and was
  never retrieved by any expand run; no exhibit page was among its five.
- **The answer was always fragile.** Without the balance sheet in context, the model infers the
  largest liability from a fair-value table on page 138 ("$123 billion") and other partial
  figures. Across runs it names customer deposits, long-term debt, or "financial liabilities
  at fair value". The 005 successes were the model guessing right from indirect evidence.
- **One of the three 007 failures is a scoring quirk.** Run 2 answered "Customer deposits were
  the largest liability…" and then appended "Not found in the filing." The refusal rule (the
  phrase anywhere means refusal) marked it a refusal and skipped the judge. A self-contradicting
  answer is arguably wrong anyway, but this is the metric, not retrieval. Across all results so
  far, 4 of 183 refusals are such hedged answers (003: 01290, 01328; 005b: 00720; 007: 01964).

01964 is noise on a question whose evidence is not retrieved. The mirror case is **00517**
(1/3 in 005 and 005b, 3/3 in 007) with identical retrieval in all three: also noise, not an
exclusion effect.

## Decision

Keep exhibit exclusion on for expansion experiments: it buys 006's evidence at 005's context
cost. `EXCLUDE_EXHIBITS` stays false by default so earlier experiments reproduce; experiments
from here on set it explicitly.

## Follow-ups

- **Refusal rule:** done (metrics v2, see README). All results were rescored: 9 records
  changed, and 01964's hedged run-2 answer is now judged correct, so 007 is 55.6% ± 1.9.
- **01964 needs page 97:** a retrieval problem (the balance sheet does not rank), a candidate
  for query rewriting.
